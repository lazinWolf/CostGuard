The judge's verdict is a model's opinion, not a certainty: it runs at temperature 0 and its reason is shown beside each answer. Use a model that answers directly (a reasoning model may spend its whole budget thinking) and is stronger than the one being checked: small local models cannot count words or follow strict rubrics, so keep rubrics to what they can read, or route the judge to a larger model through the gateway.
